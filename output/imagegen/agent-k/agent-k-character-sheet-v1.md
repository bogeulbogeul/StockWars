## 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished landscape character design sheet for AGENT K, the businesslike employee of Cipher Securities in the StockWars village game. The two reference images are STYLE AND SHEET LAYOUT references ONLY, not characters to copy. Match their clean attractive illustrated game NPC linework, readable cel shading, restrained detailing, warm ivory background, and consistent adult anatomy. This is a new character concept, not a chibi sprite or a rigged asset.
Design proposal: Korean man in his early-to-mid thirties, lean straight posture, clean-shaven angular yet approachable face, cool dark eyes, neat black side-parted hair with a single gently curved forelock, thin rectangular silver glasses with transparent lenses so expressions remain visible. Immaculate deep charcoal-navy two-piece business suit, white shirt, muted petrol teal narrow tie and small silver tie clip, black polished business shoes, discreet employee badge marked K clipped to anatomical left breast pocket. Competent reserved brokerage representative, meticulous and slightly enigmatic, never sinister or military. No sunglasses, guns, spy gadgets, gloves or combat gear.
Layout: large same-scale FULL BODY FRONT, true SIDE PROFILE, and BACK turnarounds across the left 58%, all feet visible on one baseline, exact same clothing and proportions in each view. Four generous bust expression portraits in a 2x2 grid on right: neutral professional, tiny restrained approving smile, stern warning with knitted brows, analytical concern with one hand adjusting glasses. Preserve same identity and glasses in all portraits. Bottom right a separate slim work tablet showing an abstract simple market chart and an employee ID card, plus six small palette swatches.
Text only: heading "AGENT K", small subtitle "CIPHER SECURITIES", and view labels "FRONT", "SIDE", "BACK". Airy uncluttered professional model sheet, no extra prose. Balanced fine dark outlines, crisp clean colors, broad readable shading, no painterly noise, no photorealism, no extreme anime features. The project uses RPG top-down outdoors and isometric interiors; this sheet uses standard orthographic character reference views for design review, not a claim of runtime view compatibility. Full character remains suitable for later part separation and 2D rigging, but do not draw bones or separated parts. Do not establish a global style specification.
