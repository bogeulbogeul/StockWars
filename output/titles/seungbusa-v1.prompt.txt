Title: 승부사

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a single isolated game title nameplate asset for StockWars Korean stock trading RPG. Exact Korean text "승부사" only, perfectly legible bold clean Korean sans-serif, pale warm ivory with subtle warm red tint. A compact horizontally elongated dark charcoal plaque with gently beveled clipped corners, thin refined crimson-red border, subtle highlight, no heavy texture. Left of the text, a small emblem made of exactly THREE distinct upward arrows, stepping up in height, symbolizing three consecutive profitable stock trades. Simple strong arrow silhouettes that remain clear at small sizes. Balanced generous padding, text visually centered in remaining space, readable and restrained. Width roughly 4.5 times height. Flat straight-on screen-space UI, no perspective, crisp clean contours and color areas, mild metal depth only. Actual transparent background outside the plaque, dark opaque fill inside. One asset centered, fills most of canvas width, modest transparent padding. No comparison board, no heading, no labels, no nickname, no coins, no crowns, no wings, no flames, no particles, no watermark. This is a first design candidate for the side quest '세 번의 승부', not a declaration of final global art style; pixel art is not required. The game uses RPG top-down outdoors and isometric indoors, but this overlay itself remains flat facing the viewer.
